The story

Why tsKaizen exists.

I founded tsKaizen to bring the kind of technology big companies take for granted to the local businesses that give a community its character.

My background is in finance and operations, and I have spent years building real technology that solves real problems for real people. I do not just recommend tools. I design, build, and maintain them.

What I kept noticing was the same quiet pattern everywhere: excellent businesses losing customers for reasons that had nothing to do with the quality of their work. A call at lunch nobody could take. A message at 9pm nobody saw. A quote that never got a second follow-up. None of it was a skill problem. It was a systems problem, and systems are fixable.

What drives me is the joy of building something that works and watching a business grow because of it. Innovation and your success are the same goal to me.

What you can expect

How I actually work with clients.

You talk to the builder

No account manager relaying messages to a team you never meet. The person who plans your system is the person who builds it and the person who answers when you call.

Plain language, no jargon

You should be able to explain your own system to a friend. If I cannot describe what I built without acronyms, I have not finished designing it.

Honest advice first

If the highest-impact fix is small, or something you can do yourself, that is what the plan will say. A recommendation you cannot trust is worth nothing.

Built for your reality

Systems get designed around how you actually operate, including the messy parts, not around how a demo assumes a business runs.

Local, and present

Based in Orlando, Florida. Orlando-area businesses get in-person time when it helps, and everyone else gets the same responsiveness remotely.

It keeps improving

Launch is the start, not the finish. The system gets watched, tuned, and extended, because a set-and-forget build goes stale within a year.
