About

A small Orlando studio that builds and ships.

tsKaizen was founded by Anthony Pichardo. The person who answers your quote request is the person who builds your site.

The name

Kaizen means small improvements, made continuously.

The loop in the logo is that idea: find what's slowing a business down, build something simple that fixes it, then keep improving it.

Our background is operations, making information move without someone carrying it by hand. That's meant building a full operating system for a 300 plus member organization and shipping a tournament platform used live by 30 people at once.

Today that work goes into websites for local businesses like NextGen Blinds, Wax On Wheels and For The Plot.

How we work

Three rules every project follows.

Small on purpose

No account manager and no handoff to a junior after the first email.

Simple over clever

We'd rather ship something that works on Monday than something you need training to use.

Straight answers

If you don't need what you asked for, we'll tell you before you pay for it.
