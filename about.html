The name

Kaizen means small improvements, made continuously.

The loop in the logo is that idea: find what's slowing a business down, build something simple that fixes it, then keep improving it.

My background is operations: making information move without someone carrying it by hand. Today that work goes into websites, automations and AI tools for local businesses like NextGen Blinds, Wax On Wheels and MyClearDeal.
