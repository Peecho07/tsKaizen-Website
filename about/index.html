About

A small shop that ships, not a firm that presents.

tsKaizen is an Orlando based web and AI studio run by one person. The name points at the method: find the friction, build the system, automate it, then keep improving it in small steps.

The person on your first call builds it.

tsKaizen is founded and run by Anthony Pichardo. The person you talk to on the first call is the person who designs it, builds it and hands it over. No account manager in between, and no handoff to someone you have never met.

The background is operations: process design, workflow automation, and the unglamorous work of making information move without a person carrying it. That has meant building a full operating system for a 300 plus member organization, with attendance, points and communications automated end to end, and shipping a tournament platform that ran with thirty concurrent live users.

tsKaizen applies that to small businesses. A cafe, a detail shop, a photographer and a bookstore all have the same underlying problem: too much of the week goes to work a system should be doing.
