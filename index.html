Questions owners ask first

Not covered here? Ask it on the call, or email anthonypichardo31@tskaizen.com.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the size of the site, whether your copy and photos are ready, and what it connects to, like booking or payments. After a free 30-minute call you get a written scope first, then one fixed price. You approve it before any work starts, and it does not change unless you approve a new number.

How long does it take?

Most small business sites take 2 to 3 weeks from the first call to launch. You get a live demo link partway through, so you can review it on your own phone before anything goes public.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, the code lives in a repository you own, and hosting is under your email. We only ever need access, never ownership. If you leave, we hand everything over and help you move.

I already have a website. Do I have to start over?

No. Most clients already have one. We rebuild it, carry over the copy and photos worth keeping, and switch over with minimal downtime. Your email keeps working the whole time.

Do I need the AI parts?

Probably not on day one. Most businesses get more from a better site and one automation than from a chatbot. On the call we will tell you which is which, including when the answer is none of it.

What happens after I request a call?

You get a reply by email, usually the same day, with a couple of times to talk. The call is 30 minutes. If it is a fit, a written scope and fixed price follow within a few days. If it is not, we say so and point you somewhere better.

Can I update the site myself afterward?

Yes. You get a short walkthrough at handover so you can change hours, prices and photos yourself. If you would rather not, an optional monthly care plan covers edits.

How do payments work?

Half to start and half at launch, for most projects. Monthly care plans are month to month with no contract.

Do you only work with Orlando businesses?

No. We meet in person around Central Florida and work remotely with businesses anywhere else.
