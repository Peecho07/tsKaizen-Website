Questions

Common questions

Are these prices final?

For the work listed, yes. If your project needs something outside a plan, you get a written quote for that part before anything starts. The price never grows after you approve it.

How do payments work?

Builds are half to start and half at launch. Care plans and the AI assistant bill monthly, or yearly with 2 months free.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. The quote form gives us enough to price most sites. If something is unclear, we'll ask by email. If you'd rather talk, you can ask for a call in the form.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, the code lives in an account you own, and hosting is under your email.

Do I need a care plan?

No. You own the site and can host it yourself. A care plan means you never have to think about hosting, backups or small edits.

What counts as an edit?

Changing hours, prices, photos, menu items or a paragraph of text. A brand new page or section is an extra page.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes, at the same prices. We keep the copy and photos worth keeping, and your email keeps working the whole time.

Send your current site and we'll recommend one, free.
