Questions

Common questions

Do I have to get on a call?

No. The quote form gives us enough to price most sites. If something is unclear, we'll ask by email. If you'd rather talk, you can ask for a call in the form.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, the code lives in an account you own, and hosting is under your email.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. We keep the copy and photos worth keeping, rebuild the rest, and switch over with your email working the whole time.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. You get a short walkthrough at launch. If you'd rather not, the care plan covers edits.

Do I need the add-ons?

Probably not on day one. Most businesses get the most out of a better website first. We'll tell you if an add-on is worth it for you.
