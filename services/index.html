Websites

Your site is often the first thing a customer sees, and the most common problem is not the design. It is that a person on a phone cannot tell what you do, what it costs, or how to book in the first few seconds.

Result: a fast site built around the one action you want, whether that is a call, a booking, an order or a visit.

What you get

  • Custom design, not a template with your logo dropped in
  • Built mobile first, because that is where most of your visitors are

  • Search basics done right: titles, descriptions, local business details
  • Domain, hosting and security certificate handled, in your name

Automation

The quiet time sink. A form comes in and someone copies it into a spreadsheet, then into the calendar, then writes the same follow up email again. Nobody notices it until it is gone.

Result: the steps between your tools run on their own, so the retyping stops and nothing falls through the cracks.

What you get

  • Form submissions routed to the right place automatically
  • Follow up emails and reminders that send themselves
  • Bookings, invoices and records kept in sync across tools
  • Reports that build themselves on a schedule
  • Built on the tools you already pay for where possible
  • Documented, so you are not dependent on us to change it

How the price gets set

We do not publish prices, because a five page site for a photographer and an online menu for a restaurant are not the same job. What we do publish is exactly how the number is reached.

  1. A free consultation

    Thirty minutes on what you need and what it has to connect to. Nothing is quoted on the spot and there is nothing to sign.

  2. A written scope

    Everything the build includes, listed out, so you see what you would be paying for before you see a price.

  3. One fixed price

    Quoted against that scope and held to it. You approve it before work starts, and you are free to take it elsewhere.

What moves the number

Size

How many pages, and how much of it is unique versus repeated.

Copy and photos

Whether you have them ready or we write and source them.

What it connects to

Booking, ordering, payments, a calendar, a CRM you already use.

What runs after

Automations behind the forms, and whether an assistant is part of it.

Most builds are followed by an optional monthly care plan for hosting, updates and monitoring. It is quoted alongside the project and it is never required. If the scope changes mid project, you get a new number to approve rather than a bigger invoice at the end.

Where the line is

Worth saying out loud before you hand anyone the keys to your business.

An assistant that says "I don't know"

A chatbot that invents an answer about your pricing or your hours costs you more than having no chatbot at all. Ours hand off to a person rather than guess, and you can read every conversation.

Your accounts stay yours

Domain registered in your name, code in a repository you own, hosting under your email. We never buy a domain on your behalf or hold your accounts hostage.
